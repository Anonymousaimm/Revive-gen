Good to Know

Shipping & Returns

How long does shipping take?

Orders are hand-packed within 2 business days and typically arrive within 5–7 business days domestically. International timelines vary by destination.

What's your return policy?

Unopened bottles can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened bottles are eligible for store credit — just email us and we'll take care of it.

Do you offer samples?

Yes — email us and we'll include a sample set with your first order, or send one on request before you commit to a full bottle.
